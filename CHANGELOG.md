# Changes

## 0.6.0

First public release.

- English interface and documentation.
- A compact panel with download settings under Options.
- Cache the selected video and audio with 1, 4, or 8 video connections.
- Compare the media servers supplied by the player before downloading.
- Play complete cached ranges while the remaining download continues.
- Save a completed video for seven days, renew it, or delete it.
- Remove expired copies from a cleanup worker and delete all saved copies from the toolbar popup.

This release is distributed as an unpacked extension through GitHub.
