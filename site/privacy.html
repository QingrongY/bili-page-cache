Bili Cache privacy

Updated September 28, 2026

Bili Cache runs on a video tab when you enable it. It reads that video's playback details and requests media from the video servers used by the page.

Data on your device

Temporary video data is released when you clear the cache or close the tab. If you choose Save for 7 days, the extension stores the video, title, page URL, quality and expiry date in your browser profile. You can delete saved videos from the extension popup.

Expired copies are removed during storage access, at browser startup and by a cleanup task scheduled every 30 minutes while the browser is running. The storage limit is also saved in your browser profile.

Network requests

The extension contacts Bilibili and its media servers to read playback details, test available servers and download the selected video. These services receive the usual connection information, including your IP address. Playback detail requests can use your existing Bilibili session.

Bili Cache has no analytics, advertising or developer-operated server. It does not send your video data or browsing activity to the developer.

Permissions

Active tab and scripting access let the toolbar button enable caching on the selected video tab. Alarms run saved-video cleanup when no video tab is open.
